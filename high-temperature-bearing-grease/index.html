Case Study 05 OKS Speciality Lubricants
Up to 30%

Estimated Cost Savings in High-Temperature Bearing Lubrication

A tractor manufacturing plant was reviewing lubrication performance on equipment operating in demanding, high-temperature production areas. The application called for a closer look at bearing protection and running costs.

Project Lead Dinesh Kumar
Industry
Automotive & Agricultural Equipment Manufacturing
Application
Overhead Conveyors · Hot Air Circulation Blowers
Product
OKS 9480 Synthetic High-Temperature Grease
Customer
A leading tractor manufacturer in North India
01

The Customer

The manufacturer

Tractor Manufacturing

A major tractor manufacturer operates this production facility in Punjab, where parts move through several manufacturing and finishing stages.

The process

Painting and Curing

Components pass through coating and curing operations, including cathodic electrodeposition (CED) and top-coat lines. These processes use heated ovens.

The equipment

Overhead Conveyors and HAC Blowers

Overhead conveyors carry parts through the lines. Hot air circulation (HAC) blowers help distribute heated air within the ovens.

02

The Problem

  • ABearings on the overhead conveyors and hot air circulation blowers were failing repeatedly, requiring maintenance attention.
  • BThe equipment worked in high-temperature process areas, raising questions about the suitability of the existing grease.
  • CGrease consumption was high, adding to routine lubrication costs.
03

Our Analysis

Compac’s lubrication engineers assessed the conveyor and blower applications on site to understand the operating demands, review the lubrication concerns and determine a suitable course of action.

01

Equipment Review

The team reviewed the affected conveyor trolley and HAC blower bearings, their duty conditions and the maintenance concerns reported by the plant.

02

Temperature Mapping

Compac’s lubrication engineers noted temperatures of approximately 100°C at HAC blower bearings, 125°C on the chassis conveyor line and up to 180°C in the CED and top-coat oven areas.

03

Lubrication Assessment

The engineers considered the effect of sustained heat on grease consistency, lubricant ageing and bearing protection, alongside the reported consumption and failures.

04

Application Requirements

The replacement needed an appropriate high-temperature operating range and suitability for the plant’s conveyor and blower bearing applications.

What we concluded

Given the demanding operating temperatures, reaching up to 180°C in some areas, Compac’s lubrication engineers recommended an application trial of OKS 9480, with performance monitored on the identified equipment.

04

Our Solution

Our engineers proposed OKS 9480, a synthetic high-temperature grease, for the overhead conveyor and HAC blower bearings.

Its specified operating range of −30 °C to +200 °C and stated suitability for trolley and conveyor bearings supported its selection for evaluation. Product properties are summarised alongside.

Implementation and monitoring

01

Application. OKS 9480 was applied to the selected overhead conveyor and HAC blower bearing applications.

02

Monitoring. The field trial ran for approximately two and a half months. The written report records periodic checks and satisfactory observations.

03

Close-out. The customer completed the trial report and issued a recommendation concerning additional conveyor applications.
