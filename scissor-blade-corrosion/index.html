Case Study 07 Lubeco Green Fluids

Preventing Spot Rust on Scissor Blades During Water Cooling

Scissor blades at India’s largest stationery and office hardware supplier were picking up rust during production. Compac traced the cause and resolved it within the plant’s existing process.

Project Lead Inderjit Singh Sahota
Industry
Stationery Manufacturing
Application
Corrosion Control in Post-Moulding Water Cooling
Product
Lubeco COROSEAL 33
Customer
The largest stationery and office hardware supplier in India*
01

The Customer

The manufacturer

The Largest Stationery and Office Hardware Supplier in India

The customer makes staplers, scissors and other office stationery, and has been doing so for more than six decades.

The product

Scissors with Moulded Plastic Handles

The customer’s scissors pair steel blades with plastic handles. The handles are moulded directly onto the blades during production.

The process

Water Cooling After Moulding

Once the plastic is moulded onto a blade, the part is dipped in cold water so the newly moulded plastic handle can cool.

02

The Problem

  • AWater exposure during cooling caused spot rusting on the scissor blades. The rusted parts had to be rejected, which wasted material, production time and money.
  • BBlack spots also appeared on the blade surfaces, leading to a serious quality issue.
  • CThe customer tried adding oil to the cooling water.
    The oil prevented the spot rusting but made the plastic handles extremely oily.
03

Our Analysis

01

Process Inspection

We reviewed the moulding and cooling steps, and the condition of the blades as they came out of the water.

02

Point of Intervention

The spot rusting began with the water exposure, so we identified the cooling bath as the place to act.

03

Two Surfaces in One Bath

The steel blade and the plastic handle sit in the same water, so anything added to the bath reaches both of them.

04

The Requirement

The cooling water must not have any adverse effect on the scissors.

04

Our Solution

We recommended Lubeco COROSEAL 33, a water-based, 100% synthetic corrosion inhibitor.

The updated process

01

Added at 5% concentration. COROSEAL 33 was mixed into the cooling water at a final concentration of 5%.

02

Protected the metal surface. The dissolved inhibitor formed a dry synthetic protective film under one micron thick on the metal, leaving no residue on the plastic handles.
